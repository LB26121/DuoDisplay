### Added

## [1.0] – not yet released

First version:

- one Mac as an extended or mirrored display of another over Thunderbolt, switched with ⌃⌥⌘D
- moving the pointer across the screen edge to control the other Mac
- picture-in-picture of the MacBook screen on the iMac (⌃⌥⌘P)
- MacBook sound on the iMac speakers, iMac camera and microphone as devices on the MacBook
- pairing with a code, encrypted control channel and media streams
- menu bar app in the current macOS design (Liquid Glass)
